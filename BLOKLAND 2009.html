How to Play

  • W A S D / arrows: walk   Space: jump
  • Drag mouse: camera, wheel: zoom
  • Enter: chat, Esc: menu
  • Players in the same Place see each other live (you both need the page open at the same time).
  • Builders Club: build in the Studio (left click = place, right click = delete), then Publish.
  • Crossroads: click/tap or press F to fire the slingshot. 3 hits and you get “bloxxed”!
  • Stairway to Heaven: climb the spiral, lava floor = back to your last green checkpoint.
  • Avoid the red lava, collect Tix, finish the obby!

How to build
1. Insert menu (or ▢ ⬤ 🔥 buttons) puts a new brick in front of you.
2. Click a brick (or its name in Explorer) to select it.
3. Pick Move, Resize or Rotate and press the buttons under the toolbar.
4. Walk with W A S D, drag to look around.
5. Type a Place name and press 🌐 to publish.
